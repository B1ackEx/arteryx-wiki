    Custom Features

    ArteryX adds a collection of quality-of-life features that improve survival without changing the vanilla experience.

    🦇 Bat Membranes

    Phantoms are disabled as a membrane source. Bats can drop Phantom Membranes instead, making them a renewable resource.

    🏗️ Concrete Cauldron

    Right-click Concrete Powder on a Water Cauldron to instantly create Concrete.

    🟢 Slime Cauldron

    Special Slime Cauldrons allow you to use Slime Blocks to age Copper.

    🍯 Honey Cauldron

    Special Honey Cauldrons allow you to use Honey Blocks to wax Copper.

    🟫 Mud Cauldron

    Right-click Dirt on a Water Cauldron to convert it into Mud.

    💥 Confetti Creepers

    Some Creepers become Confetti Creepers, preventing block damage from their explosion.

    • 30% chance
    • 50% reduced entity damage
    • No block damage

    🐉 Dragon Rewards

    Every Ender Dragon has a 25% chance to drop an Elytra.

    🪶 Husk Sand Drops

    Husks can drop Sand, making Sand renewable through mob farming.

    🧟 Infested Villagers

    Villagers affected by the Infested effect can transform into Zombie Villagers after a short period.

    • Minimum: 60 seconds
    • Maximum: 180 seconds

    🖼️ Better Paintings

    Use a Stonecutter to choose between different Painting variants before placing them.

    🖌️ Invisible Item Frames

    Use a Brush on an Item Frame to toggle its visibility.

    🤫 Silent Mobs

    Rename any mob:

    Silence Me

    or

    silence_me

    to permanently silence it.

    🪚 Wooden Stonecutter

    The Stonecutter also works as a Sawmill, allowing more efficient wooden recipes.

    📖 Auto Unlock Recipes

    All crafting recipes are unlocked automatically when you join.

    🐺 Transfer Pets

    Safely transfer ownership of tamed pets to another player using leads.

    🐣 Keep Baby Animals Small

    Name a baby mob:

    Keep Small

    to keep it permanently small.

    Rename it:

    Grow

    to allow it to become an adult.

    🧱 Mini Blocks

    Wandering Traders can sell decorative Mini Blocks for builders.

    ⚒️ Craftable Horse Armor

    Horse Armor can be crafted instead of relying entirely on exploration and loot.

    🪑 Craftable Saddles

    Saddles can be crafted, making them easier to obtain during survival.

    🌱 Small Dripleaf

    Craft Small Dripleaf from Large Dripleaf for easier decoration and building.

    🌸 Craftable Spore Blossoms

    Spore Blossoms can be crafted using Lilacs, Azalea Leaves, and Honeycombs.

    🟨 Craftable Gilded Blackstone

    Gilded Blackstone can be crafted using Gold Ingots and Blackstone.

    ⛏️ Smeltable Raw Ore Blocks

    Raw Ore Blocks can be smelted directly into their respective Ore Blocks.

    🔩 Unsticky Pistons

    Right-click a piston with an Axe to remove slime from it.

    Right-click a piston with a Slimeball to make it sticky.

    🧪 XP Bottling

    Store your experience using Glass Bottles.

    • Costs 10 XP to bottle
    • Returns 50 XP when the bottle is used
    • Sneak to bottle multiple bottles at once

    🏆 Vanilla, But Better

    Every feature is designed to save time, make resources more accessible, and improve survival while keeping ArteryX feeling close to vanilla Minecraft.
