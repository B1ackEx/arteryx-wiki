    🎣 Custom Fishing

    Fishing on ArteryX is more than catching vanilla fish.

    The Custom Fishing system adds custom fish, fishing levels, Entropy, Augments, Deliveries, Totems, Scales, Gutting, Baits, Skills, Fishing Tournaments, special fish, custom fishing mobs, and more.

    Whether you want to make money, level up your fishing, collect rare fish, compete in tournaments, or build the strongest fishing rod possible, there is always something to work toward.

    6 Tiers

    🎣 Getting Started

    The easiest way to begin is simply to fish.

    Use:

    /fish menu

    to open the main fishing menu.

    You can also use the individual fishing commands explained throughout this guide.

    You will need:

    • A fishing rod
    • A place where you can fish
    • Space in your inventory for catches
    • Patience
    • A willingness to build your fishing setup over time

    As you fish, you will catch Custom Fish.

    Custom Fish are different from normal Minecraft fish and are the main progression item for ArteryX's fishing system.

    Higher-tier fish are rarer and generally provide better rewards.

    🐟 Custom Fish Tiers

    ArteryX uses six Custom Fish tiers.

    Tier Rarity General Purpose
    🥉 Bronze Common Starter fishing
    🥈 Silver Uncommon Early progression
    🥇 Gold Rare Mid-game fishing
    💎 Diamond Very Rare Advanced fishing
    💠 Platinum Extremely Rare High-end fishing
    🌠 Mythical Legendary End-game fishing

    Your fishing progression is not only about money.

    Higher-tier fish can also provide more:

    • Entropy
    • Fishing XP
    • Delivery value
    • Collection progress
    • Tournament potential

    💰 Fishing Money

    Custom Fish can be sold through the fishing shop.

    Use:

    /fish shop

    to open the Fishing Shop.

    From there, you can sell eligible fish and receive money.

    Warning

    ArteryX has a $50,000 daily fishing money limit.

    Once you reach the daily limit, you cannot continue generating fishing money until the limit resets.

    The limit is designed to keep fishing profitable without allowing it to completely replace the rest of the ArteryX economy.

    🔮 Entropy

    Entropy is the main special currency used by the fishing system.

    Unlike normal money, Entropy is mainly used for fishing progression.

    It is used for things such as:

    • Crafting Augments
    • Upgrading fishing progression
    • Fishing system upgrades
    • Other advanced fishing features

    You can check your fishing statistics using:

    /fish stats

    Entropy can be earned in several ways.

    🎣 Catching Fish

    Catching Custom Fish gives Entropy.

    Generally, rarer fish provide more Entropy.

    ⚔️ Gutting Fish

    You can sacrifice Custom Fish for Entropy using:

    /fish gut

    This permanently destroys the fish.

    📦 Deliveries

    Completing fishing Deliveries can provide Entropy along with other rewards.

    🏆 Tournaments

    Fishing Tournaments can also provide Entropy and other rewards.

    👥 Fishing With Others

    Party Fishing can provide additional benefits when multiple players fish together.

    ⚔️ Gutting Fish

    Gutting is a way to turn fish you do not want to keep into Entropy.

    Open:

    /fish gut

    or access the Gutting Station through the fishing menu.

    Place your fish inside the menu and confirm by closing the menu.

    The fish will be destroyed and you will receive its Entropy reward.

    Warning

    Gutting is permanent.

    Once a fish has been gutted, you cannot recover it.

    Before gutting valuable fish, check whether the fish could be more useful for:

    • Deliveries
    • Selling
    • Tournaments
    • Collection
    • Trading

    🔪 Precision Cutting

    The Precision Cutting Augment improves the amount of Entropy you receive when gutting fish.

    This makes it particularly useful if your main goal is building an Entropy-heavy fishing setup.

    For the best results, combine your fishing progression with:

    • Precision Cutting
    • Fishing Skills
    • Entropy bonuses
    • High-tier fish

    ⚖️ Fish Scales

    Scales are a risk-versus-reward feature.

    Use:

    /fish scales

    to open the Scales menu.

    You place a Custom Fish into the menu and choose a risk level.

    The system then changes the fish's selling value.

    You can potentially increase the value of your fish, but you can also lose value.

    Risk Levels

    Risk Chance of Profit Possible Change
    🪶 Low 70% ±20%
    ⚙️ Medium 60% ±40%
    🔥 High 50% ±60%
    💀 Extreme 40% ±80%

    Low

    The safest option.

    You have the highest chance of increasing the fish's value, but the possible increase is smaller.

    Medium

    A balanced option.

    You accept more risk for a potentially larger increase.

    High

    A risky option for players who are willing to gamble more of the fish's value.

    Extreme

    The biggest gamble.

    You have the lowest chance of a profitable result, but the potential change in value is much larger.

    Warning

    Do not scale valuable fish blindly.

    A bad roll can reduce the value of your fish.

    🧩 Augments

    Augments are special upgrades for your fishing rod.

    They work similarly to custom fishing enchantments.

    You can view the available Augments with:

    /fish augments

    Augments allow you to specialize your rod for different playstyles.

    Some focus on:

    • More fish
    • Better fish
    • More Entropy
    • More XP
    • More money
    • Better gutting
    • Better custom mob chances
    • Better Scales results
    • Special weather effects

    🔨 How To Craft An Augment

    Augments are crafted using their required ingredients and Entropy.

    The general process is:

    1. Check the Augment

    Use:

    /fish augments

    Find the Augment you want and check its requirements.

    2. Get The Required Materials

    Every Augment has its own recipe.

    Recipes can require normal Minecraft items as well as special fishing items.

    3. Make Sure You Have Enough Entropy

    Entropy is taken from your fishing balance when crafting.

    4. Craft The Augment

    Follow the recipe shown by the fishing system.

    Once crafted, the Augment becomes available to apply to your fishing rod.

    🎣 Applying Augments

    Use:

    /fish augment

    The Augment menu allows you to apply your crafted Augments to a fishing rod.

    Place:

    • Your fishing rod
    • The Augment

    into the appropriate GUI slots.

    Confirm the operation.

    Your rod will receive the Augment.

    🧹 Removing Augments

    If you want to remove Augments from your rod, use the Augment menu.

    Removing Augments costs Entropy.

    Warning

    Removing an Augment is not something you should do randomly.

    Plan your rod before investing heavily into Augments.

    Hot Spot gives you a chance to catch additional fish.

    Higher levels increase the chance.

    Configured progression:

    Level 1  → 6%
Level 2  → 11%
Level 3  → 15%
Level 4  → 19%
Level 5  → 25%

    Additional levels continue through the configured Hot Spot progression.

    Hot Spot is a strong choice for players who want:

    • More fish
    • More Entropy
    • More delivery progress
    • More chances at rare catches

    ⛈️ Call of the Storm

    Call of the Storm improves your fishing while it is raining.

    Configured values:

    Level 1 → 5%
Level 2 → 15%
Level 3 → 25%
Level 4 → 35%
Level 5 → 50%

    This is particularly useful for players who want to build around weather-based fishing.

    🍗 Saturate

    Saturate gives you a chance to restore hunger while fishing.

    Configured values:

    Level 1 → 5%
Level 2 → 10%
Level 3 → 20%
Level 4 → 30%
Level 5 → 50%

    This is primarily a quality-of-life Augment.

    🌎 Biome Disruption

    Biome Disruption gives you a chance to catch fish from other biome pools.

    Configured values:

    Level 1 → 15%
Level 2 → 40%
Level 3 → 65%

    This is extremely useful when you are hunting fish that normally require different fishing environments.

    🔪 Precision Cutting

    Precision Cutting improves the Entropy earned from gutting fish.

    It is designed for players who frequently use:

    /fish gut

    If your goal is to generate large amounts of Entropy, this is one of the most important Augments to consider.

    🦀 Crab Bait

    Crab Bait increases your chance of encountering fishing-related custom mobs such as Crabs.

    Crabs are important because their drops can be used throughout the fishing progression system.

    🧠 Intellect

    Intellect improves the XP you gain from fishing.

    This is useful when your main objective is leveling rather than directly maximizing money.

    👁️ Perception

    Perception improves the Entropy gained from catching fish.

    This makes it especially useful for players who want to build an Entropy-focused rod without relying entirely on gutting.

    🏆 Trophy

    Trophy improves your results when using the Scales system.

    If you frequently use:

    /fish scales

    Trophy becomes more valuable because it improves the effectiveness of your scaling strategy.

    ☀️ Solar Rage

    Solar Rage improves the money generated from selling fish.

    This is one of the most direct money-focused Augments.

    It pairs naturally with:

    • High-tier fish
    • Scales
    • Fishing Shop
    • Fishing money limits

    🎣 Master Fisherman

    Master Fisherman is a late-game fishing Augment designed to improve your overall fishing performance and chances of obtaining higher-tier fish.

    It requires a significantly higher Fishing Level and Entropy investment than early-game Augments.

    🪱 Baits

    Baits are specialized fishing items that can be used to focus your fishing setup toward different fish groups.

    ArteryX currently includes multiple bait types.

    Bait Unlock Level Uses
    Starter Bait 1 50
    Freshwater Bait 5 50
    Ocean Bait 10 50
    Rare Bait 20 50
    Frozen Bait 25 50
    Deepsea Bait 30 50
    Nether Bait 35 50
    Legendary Bait 45 40
    Celestial Bait 60 30

    Baits are designed to help you target different groups of fish instead of simply fishing randomly.

    📦 Deliveries

    Deliveries are fishing orders.

    They give you a reason to keep your fish instead of immediately selling or gutting everything you catch.

    Use:

    /fish deliveries

    to access the Delivery system.

    📦 How Deliveries Work

    The basic process is:

    Catch Custom Fish
        ↓
Unlock Delivery
        ↓
Check Required Fish
        ↓
Collect The Required Fish
        ↓
Start The Delivery
        ↓
Wait For Completion
        ↓
Collect Your Rewards

    ArteryX requires 150 Custom Fish to progress to a new Delivery.

    Warning

    Only Custom Fish count toward Delivery progression.

    Normal vanilla fish do not count.

    🐟 Why You Should Save Fish

    It can be tempting to sell every fish you catch.

    However, Deliveries may require specific fish.

    Before selling or gutting large quantities of fish, check your current Delivery.

    Sometimes keeping a fish is more valuable than immediately selling it.

    ⏱️ Delivery Times

    Different Delivery types have different completion times.

    The configured timers include:

    Delivery Time
    1 30 seconds
    2 1 minute
    3 2 minutes
    4 1 minute
    5 2 minutes
    6 4 minutes
    7 15 minutes

    The required fish and rewards depend on the Delivery.

    🎁 Delivery Rewards

    Deliveries can reward you with special fishing items.

    Rewards can include:

    • 🦀 Crab Claws
    • 🦀 Crab Scales
    • 🐬 Dolphin Tails
    • 🦑 Squid Tentacles
    • 🔮 Entropy Crystals
    • 🎣 Platinum Lures
    • 🦀 Crab Lures
    • 🌠 Mythical Lures

    Some deliveries provide stronger rewards than others.

    🔮 Entropy Crystals

    Entropy Crystals are physical items representing stored Entropy.

    They are useful when you want to:

    • Store Entropy
    • Trade Entropy
    • Move Entropy between players
    • Receive Entropy as a Delivery reward

    🗼 Fishing Totems

    Fishing Totems are a mid-to-late-game fishing progression system.

    They provide passive bonuses while you are fishing within their effective area.

    The Totem system allows you to build a more advanced fishing setup rather than relying only on your fishing rod.

    🧱 Building A Fishing Totem

    Fishing Totems require special fishing materials.

    The configured Totem system uses items such as:

    • Dolphin Tails
    • Crab Claws
    • Crab Scales
    • Squid Tentacles
    • High-tier fish

    A Fishing Totem becomes available at the required Fishing Level.

    Note

    The Totem is intended as a progression feature, so you should not expect to build one immediately after joining the server.

    ⚙️ Totem Upgrades

    Totems can provide multiple upgradeable properties.

    The important upgrade categories are:

    📏 Radius

    Controls how far the Totem's effects can reach.

    A larger radius means more area in which the Totem can benefit your fishing.

    ⏱️ Cooldown

    Reduces the time between Totem activations/effects.

    ⌛ Active Time

    Increases how long the Totem remains active.

    The exact upgrade cost and level information can be viewed through the Totem system in-game.

    🌟 Totem Passives

    Totem Passives are additional bonuses that can be added to your Fishing Totem.

    ArteryX currently uses:

    Passive Unlock Level Slots Purpose
    🎓 Experienced Fisherman 20 1 Increased Fishing XP
    🦀 Little Critters 40 2 Improves custom mob benefits
    🐟 Fish School 40 3 Improves fishing bonuses
    🎁 Random Drops 55 4 Chance for additional mob drops
    💎 Treasure Hunter 60 3 Chance for valuable loot
    🌊 Mythical Waters 60 5 Chance to improve fish tier
    🌀 Entropy Hoarder 60 6 Increased Entropy

    🎓 Experienced Fisherman

    Provides a Fishing XP bonus while its Totem effect is active.

    This is particularly useful when leveling your Fishing Level.

    🦀 Little Critters

    Improves the benefits associated with fishing-related custom mobs.

    This is useful for players who actively hunt for:

    • Crab Claws
    • Crab Scales
    • Dolphin Tails
    • Squid Tentacles

    🐟 Fish School

    Improves fishing-related bonus effects.

    This passive works well with a fishing setup focused on catching more fish.

    🎁 Random Drops

    Gives you a chance to receive additional fishing-related mob drops.

    This can help supply the materials required for other fishing progression systems.

    💎 Treasure Hunter

    Provides a chance to receive valuable additional loot while fishing.

    It is aimed at players who want extra rewards beyond the fish themselves.

    🌊 Mythical Waters

    Provides a chance to improve the tier of fish you catch.

    This makes it particularly valuable for players hunting:

    • Platinum
    • Mythical
    • Other high-tier fish

    🌀 Entropy Hoarder

    Increases the amount of Entropy you receive.

    If your primary goal is building expensive Augments and progressing through fishing systems, this is one of the most useful Totem Passives.

    🏆 Fishing Tournaments

    Fishing Tournaments are competitive events where players compete against each other.

    Tournament types can include objectives such as:

    • Catching the most fish
    • Catching the longest fish
    • Catching the shortest fish
    • Earning the most Entropy
    • Catching specific fish
    • Catching fish from different biomes
    • Longest casts
    • Total fish length

    Each tournament has its own objective.

    🥇 Tournament Rewards

    ArteryX's configured rewards are:

    🥇 1st Place

    2x Arcane Shard
$5,000

    🥈 2nd Place

    1x Arcane Shard
$3,000

    🥉 3rd Place

    Fishing Crate Key
$2,000

    Participants can also receive an Entropy reward for taking part.

    👥 Party Fishing

    Fishing does not have to be a solo activity.

    Party Fishing rewards players for fishing near each other.

    The configured system uses:

    Maximum players for full bonus: 5
Radius: 40 blocks

    This means you can fish together with other players and benefit from the Party Fishing system.

    It is a great way to turn fishing into a social activity.

    🦀 Custom Fishing Mobs

    Fishing can also trigger special custom mobs.

    ArteryX currently uses custom fishing encounters such as:

    🦀 Crab

    Crabs can provide:

    • Crab Claws
    • Crab Scales

    These materials are useful for fishing progression.

    🐬 Dolphin

    Dolphins can provide:

    • Dolphin Tails

    🦑 Squid

    Squids can provide:

    • Squid Tentacles

    These materials can be used for fishing-related recipes, Deliveries and progression.

    🎯 Fishing Skills

    Fishing Skills provide additional progression alongside your Fishing Level.

    Skill Points can be invested into fishing abilities.

    Skills can improve different parts of the fishing system, such as:

    • Gutting
    • Entropy
    • Augments
    • Totems
    • Catching
    • Special fishing effects

    Use the Fishing Skills menu to see the skills available to your character.

    The fishing system is designed to grow with you.

    💡 Fishing Tips

    💰 Don't Sell Everything

    Keep fish required for Deliveries.

    🔮 Don't Waste Entropy

    Save Entropy for useful Augments and progression.

    🐟 Chase Rare Fish

    Higher-tier fish are generally more valuable and useful.

    ⚖️ Use Scales Carefully

    The bigger the possible reward, the bigger the possible loss.

    🔪 Gut Unwanted Fish

    Gutting is useful when you need additional Entropy.

    🗼 Build A Totem

    Totems become extremely valuable as you progress into higher Fishing Levels.

    🏆 Watch For Tournaments

    Tournaments provide another reason to keep fishing and can provide valuable rewards.

    👥 Fish Together

    Party Fishing makes fishing more rewarding when multiple players fish nearby.

    ❓ Quick FAQ

    Does normal Minecraft fish count toward Deliveries?

    No.

    Deliveries use Custom Fish progression.

    What is Entropy?

    Entropy is the special currency used by the Custom Fishing progression system.

    Can I recover a fish after gutting it?

    No. Gutting permanently destroys the fish.

    What is the safest Scales option?

    Low Risk has the highest configured chance of a positive result.

    What are Augments?

    Augments are special fishing-rod upgrades that modify how your fishing works.

    What are Totems?

    Totems are advanced fishing structures that provide passive bonuses while fishing within their effect area.

    Use:

# 🐟 All Custom Fish

Welcome to the ArteryX Custom Fish collection.

Every Custom Fish belongs to a specific tier.

Use:

```text
/fish codex

    in-game to discover fish and learn more about your catches.

    This page lists the Custom Fish currently available on ArteryX.

    🥉 Bronze Fish

    Bronze is the most common Custom Fish tier.

    These fish are the foundation of your fishing progression and are commonly encountered while starting out.

    Fish Tier
    🐟 River Pike Bronze
    ✨ Glow Minnow Bronze
    🟤 Copperfin Bronze
    🌿 Marsh Perch Bronze

    Bronze fish are useful for:

    • Early money
    • Entropy
    • Deliveries
    • Fishing XP
    • Learning the fishing system

    Do not ignore Bronze fish just because they are common.

    You will need large numbers of Custom Fish as you progress through Deliveries.

    🥈 Silver Fish

    Silver fish are less common than Bronze fish and provide better progression value.

    Fish Tier
    🌙 Moon Koi Silver
    🫧 Pearlfin Silver
    ❄️ Frostscale Silver
    ⚡ Storm Eel Silver

    Silver fish are useful for:

    • Better fishing income
    • More Entropy
    • Deliveries
    • Progressing toward stronger fishing setups

    🥇 Gold Fish

    Gold fish are rare catches and mark the beginning of more advanced fishing.

    Fish Tier
    🟡 Golden Koi Gold
    🔥 Emberfin Gold
    🦷 Deepfang Gold
    ☀️ Sunray Fish Gold

    Gold fish are valuable for players progressing toward advanced fishing systems.

    💎 Diamond Fish

    Diamond fish are very rare.

    Fish Tier
    ❄️ Frostfire Fish Diamond
    🌊 Abyssfin Diamond
    💠 Prism Koi Diamond
    ⚡ Stormray Diamond

    Diamond fish are excellent catches for:

    • Money
    • Entropy
    • Deliveries
    • Collection
    • Fishing progression

    💠 Platinum Fish

    Platinum fish are among the rarest fish available through normal fishing.

    Fish Tier
    🌌 Voidfin Platinum
    🔥 Inferno Koi Platinum
    ✨ Celestial Ray Platinum
    🦈 Emperor Shark Platinum

    Finding a Platinum fish should feel like a significant catch.

    Keep them safe until you decide whether you want to:

    • Sell them
    • Use them for a Delivery
    • Use them for fishing progression
    • Keep them as collectibles
    • Use them with other fishing systems

    🌠 Mythical Fish

    Mythical is the highest Custom Fish tier.

    These are extremely rare catches intended to be exciting end-game discoveries.

    Fish Tier
    🐉 Arteryx Leviathan Mythical
    🐍 Abyssal Serpent Mythical
    ☀️ Solaris Fish Mythical
    🔥 Netherfin Mythical

    Finding a Mythical fish is one of the biggest moments in the Custom Fishing system.

    If you catch one, do not immediately sell or gut it without checking whether you need it for another part of the fishing system.

    📊 Fish Tier Overview

    Tier Difficulty Progression
    🥉 Bronze Common Starter
    🥈 Silver Uncommon Early
    🥇 Gold Rare Mid
    💎 Diamond Very Rare Advanced
    💠 Platinum Extremely Rare End-Game
    🌠 Mythical Legendary Highest

    🎯 Why Fish Tiers Matter

    Fish tiers affect how valuable a catch can be throughout the fishing system.

    Higher-tier fish are generally more desirable because they can provide better:

    • Money
    • Entropy
    • Fishing XP
    • Collection value
    • Delivery value
    • Tournament potential

    However, rarer does not always mean you should sell it immediately.

    A rare fish may be more useful for a Delivery or another fishing objective.

    📦 Fish & Deliveries

    Some Deliveries require specific Custom Fish.

    Before selling or gutting a rare fish, check:

    /fish deliveries

    If your current Delivery needs that fish, keeping it may be the better choice.

    ⚖️ Fish & Scales

    Fish can be placed into:

    /fish scales

    to attempt to change their selling value.

    The risk increases as you move from:

    Low
↓
Medium
↓
High
↓
Extreme

    Rare fish can produce larger monetary results, but they can also become less valuable after a bad Scales result.

    🔪 Fish & Gutting

    Any fish that is eligible for gutting can be converted into Entropy.

    Use:

    /fish gut

    Remember:

    Gutting destroys the fish.

    Do not gut a rare fish before checking whether you need it elsewhere.

    🏆 Fish & Tournaments

    Some Fishing Tournaments can use fish-related objectives.

    Depending on the tournament type, players may compete around:

    • Fish count
    • Fish length
    • Specific fish
    • Total length
    • Entropy
    • Other fishing statistics

    This means even a fish that you would normally sell may become useful during a tournament.

    🗼 Fish & Totems

    Some fish and fishing-mob drops are useful for Totem progression.

    Special fishing materials can be required when building or progressing your Fishing Totem.

    This is another reason not to treat every fish-related item as simple sellable loot.

    🧪 Fish-Related Special Drops

    Custom Fishing also includes special fishing-related items obtained through custom mobs and other systems.

    Important materials include:

    Item Main Use
    🦀 Crab Claw Fishing progression
    🦀 Crab Scale Fishing progression
    🐬 Dolphin Tail Fishing progression
    🦑 Squid Tentacle Fishing progression
    💎 Entropy Crystal Stored Entropy

    These materials can be important for:

    • Augments
    • Deliveries
    • Totems
    • Other fishing progression

    🔍 How To Discover Fish

    The easiest way to explore the collection is:

    /fish codex

    The Codex is your in-game fishing encyclopedia.

    Use it to discover fish, check their information, and work toward completing your collection.
