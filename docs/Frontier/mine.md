The **Forsaken Frontier** is a dangerous collection of mines and forgotten lands waiting to be explored. Each region brings its own hostile mobs, valuable drops, rare fragments, and powerful enemies.

From the burning depths of the Nether to the forgotten chambers of the Deep Dark, every expedition offers new challenges and rewards for those brave enough to enter.

### 👑 Boss: Infernal Warden

The **Infernal Warden** is the major boss of the Infernal Mines. This powerful enemy uses devastating attacks and becomes more dangerous as the fight progresses.

<div class="feature-card">

<div class="emoji">🔥</div>

<h3>Infernal Warden</h3>

<p>Prepare for a dangerous encounter against one of the Frontier's most powerful enemies.</p>

<ul>

<li>500 health</li>

<li>19 damage</li>

<li>Hellfire Slam, Roar, Fire Ring, and Infernal Strike abilities</li>

<li>Enters Phase Two at 50% health</li>

<li>Can summon minions and use Meteor attacks in Phase Two</li>

<li>Grants 180 experience and $5,000 on defeat</li>

</ul>

</div>

### 🎁 Infernal Warden Rewards

Defeating the Warden can reward players with:

* **Infernal Cores** — the main boss fragment reward
* **Infernal Sword** — a rare weapon drop
* **Infernal Armor** — helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots
* **Nether Boss Head** — a rare trophy drop
* **15 Gold Blocks** — a guaranteed reward from the supplied drop configuration

The Warden is a valuable target for players looking to obtain powerful equipment and rare boss loot.

## 💎 Fragments & Rare Drops

Fragments are among the important rewards found throughout the Frontier. Different areas and enemies provide different types, giving players reasons to explore beyond the normal mines.

| Fragment          | Source                              |
| :---------------- | :---------------------------------- |
| Zombie Fragment   | Forsaken Miner and Ancient Sentinel |
| Hellfire Fragment | Infernal Mines enemies              |
| Slime Fragment    | Trial Master                        |
| Void Shard        | Ancient Summoner                    |
| Infernal Core     | Infernal Warden                     |

Some enemies can also drop rare equipment, weapons, keys, and collectible mob heads.

## ⚔️ Prepare for the Frontier

Before entering a dangerous area:

* Bring suitable armor and weapons.
* Prepare for hostile mobs and powerful attacks.
* Watch for status effects such as Darkness, Weakness, and Slowness.
* Fight dangerous enemies with care.
* Collect fragments and rare equipment to strengthen your progression.

The Forsaken Frontier rewards preparation, exploration, and persistence. Enter the mines, challenge their guardians, and claim the treasures hidden across ArteryX.
