# Forge Spawners

Forge Spawners are one of the fastest ways to build fully automated farms and generate a steady income.

Unlike vanilla, every player can eventually obtain and move spawners.

---

## 🛒 Getting a Spawner

There are two ways to obtain a spawner.

### ⛏ Mine One

Spawners can be mined using a **Silk Touch Pickaxe**.

---

### 💰 Buy One

Purchase any spawner directly using:

```text
/spawner get
```

Price:

```text
$75,000
```

---

## ⚡ Upgrading Your Farm

Spawners become even more powerful when combined with:

- ⚙ Flux Hoppers
- 💸 Sell Wands
- 📦 Large Storage Systems

This allows you to create completely automated money farms.

---

## 📦 Supported Mobs

Most vanilla mob spawners are supported.

Common choices include:

- Zombie
- Skeleton
- Spider
- Blaze
- Breeze
- Cave Spider
- Pig
- Cow
- Chicken
- Sheep

More mob types may become available through future updates.

---

## 💡 Tips

- Place your spawners inside a claimed area.
- Use Flux Hoppers to automatically collect drops.
- Use Sell Wands to instantly sell your farm's loot.
- Upgrade your farm over time for maximum profit.

---

!!! tip "Best Combo"

    Forge Spawner + Flux Hopper + Sell Wand = One of the easiest ways to earn money on ArteryX.
