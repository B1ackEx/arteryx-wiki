# Seasons

ArteryX features a fully dynamic **Season System** that changes the world throughout the year.

Each season brings unique weather, lighting, particles, decorations, mob behavior, and exclusive rewards—making survival feel fresh every time you play.

---

## 🕒 Day & Night Length

Unlike vanilla Minecraft, every season has different daylight hours.

| Season | Day | Night |
|:--|:--:|:--:|
| 🌸 Spring | Medium | Medium |
| ☀️ Summer | Long | Short |
| 🍂 Autumn | Medium | Medium |
| ❄️ Winter | Short | Long |

## 👾 Seasonal Mobs

Some creatures become more common depending on the current season.

| Season | Changes |
|:--|:--|
| 🌸 Spring | More Bees & passive mobs |
| ☀️ Summer | Husks replace Zombies |
| 🍂 Autumn | More Foxes & Bats, Pumpkin mobs |
| ❄️ Winter | Strays replace Skeletons |

## 🎁 Seasonal Crates

Every season introduces its own **exclusive crate**.

At the beginning of each new season, every player receives:

🎁 **1 Seasonal Crate Key**

Current seasonal crates include:

- 🌸 Spring Crate
- ☀️ Summer Crate
- 🍂 Autumn Crate
- ❄️ Winter Crate

Each crate contains exclusive seasonal rewards that are only available during that season.

Open them anytime at:

```text
/warp crates
```
