# 🏝️ OneBlock Islands

Your island is the heart of your ArteryX journey. Build, expand, progress through OneBlock phases, create farms, and turn your island into an empire.

---

## 🧱 Creating Your Island

Create your island with:

```text
/ob create
```

Every player starts with a **50 × 50** island.

Your island is your personal protected space where you can build, farm, automate, and progress without worrying about other players destroying your work.

---

## 📏 Island Upgrades

Need more room?

Use:

```text
/ob upgrade
```

Your island starts at:

**50 × 50**

and can be upgraded all the way to:

**250 × 250**

Upgrading your island gives you more room for massive builds, farms, storage systems, mob farms, and automation.

Your upgraded island size also applies to your **Nether and End islands**.

---

## 🧱 OneBlock Phases

Your OneBlock changes as you progress through different phases.

View your unlocked phases with:

```text
/ob phases
```

You can switch between **any phases you have already unlocked**, allowing you to return to previous phases whenever you need specific resources.

Progress through phases to unlock new blocks, ores, mobs, resources, and other content.

---

## ⚙️ Island Settings

Customize how your island works with:

```text
/ob settings
```

Depending on your permissions and available settings, you can manage different island flags and gameplay options.

This is where you can control various interactions and island-related settings.

---

## 👥 Island Members

Playing alone isn't as fun.

Invite upto max 4 friends and build together on your island. Your island members can help with building, farms, automation, and progression.

Only invite players you trust.

---

## 🌀 Island Warp

Want players to visit your island?

Use:

```text
/ob warp
```

Your island warp allows other players to visit your island without needing to manually navigate to it.

Perfect for showcasing your builds, shops, farms, or island projects.

---

## 📊 Island Level

Want to see how developed your island is?

Use:

```text
/ob level
```

Your island level represents the value and progression of your island.

Build with valuable blocks, expand your island, and create impressive structures to increase your island level.

---

## 🔥 Nether Island

ArteryX also features a dedicated **Nether island**.

The Nether island is a void environment connected to your main island progression, giving you a dedicated space for Nether builds, farms, and resources.

Your Nether island uses the **same size as your main island**.

For example:

```text
Overworld → 100 × 100
Nether    → 100 × 100
```

---

## 🐉 End Island

The End also has its own dedicated **void island**.

Use it for End progression, farms, builds, and late-game projects.

Just like the Nether, its size matches your main island:

```text
Overworld → 150 × 150
Nether    → 150 × 150
End       → 150 × 150
```

Upgrade your island to increase the available space across your dimensions.

---

## 🛡️ Island Protection

Your island is protected from unauthorized players.

Other players cannot freely:

* ❌ Break your blocks
* ❌ Place blocks
* ❌ Destroy your builds
* ❌ Steal from your island
* ❌ Interfere with your farms

You control who can access and interact with your island.

---

## 🎒 Keep Inventory

**Keep Inventory is disabled on ArteryX.**

Dying can cause you to lose the items you're carrying, so store valuable resources safely before taking unnecessary risks.

!!! tip "Useful Commands"

```
`/ob` — View available OneBlock commands  
`/ob create` — Create your island  
`/ob settings` — Manage island settings and flags  
`/ob phases` — View and switch between unlocked phases  
`/ob upgrade` — Upgrade your island size  
`/ob warp` — Manage your island warp  
`/ob level` — View your island level
```

!!! info "Island Size"

```
Islands start at 50 × 50 and can be upgraded up to **250 × 250**. Your Nether and End islands follow the same size.
```

!!! warning "Death"

```
Keep Inventory is disabled. Store valuable items before heading into dangerous areas.
```
