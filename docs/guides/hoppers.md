# Flux Hopper

Flux Hoppers are upgraded hoppers designed to automate your farms and reduce manual work.

They collect nearby drops, transfer items faster than vanilla hoppers, and can send items directly into linked containers.

## 📈 Upgrade System

Each Flux Hopper can be upgraded.

Upgradeable stats include:

- 📦 Transfer Amount
- 🧲 Suction Radius
- 🔗 Link Amount
- 📍 Link Distance

Open the hopper menu after placing it to upgrade these stats.

---

## 🔗 Linking Containers

Flux Hoppers can connect directly to nearby storage.

Simply:

1. Place your Flux Hopper.
2. Open its menu.
3. Click **Links**.
4. Link nearby chests, barrels, or other containers.

This allows farms to stay fully automated without overflowing.

---

## 💰 Farm Automation

Flux Hoppers work perfectly with:

- 🌾 Crop Farms
- 🐄 Animal Farms

## ⭐ Why Use Flux Hoppers?

Compared to vanilla hoppers, Flux Hoppers offer:

- Faster item transfer
- Larger pickup radius
- Container linking
- Upgradeable performance
- Better server performance
- Cleaner automated farms

---

## 💡 Tips

- Upgrade the suction radius first for better collection.
- Link multiple storage containers before increasing transfer speed.
- Keep your hopper inside your claim to prevent griefing.
