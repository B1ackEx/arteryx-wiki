# Claim Blocks

Claim Blocks protect your builds from griefing and unauthorized players.

ArteryX uses **GriefPrevention** to keep your land safe.

---

## Starting Claim Blocks

Every new player starts with:

🟨 **1,536 Claim Blocks**

This is enough to protect approximately **6 chunks**.

---

## Where Can I Claim?

Claims can only be created in the:

🌍 **Overworld**

Claims are not available in other worlds.

---

## Minimum Claim Size

Your first claim must be at least:

📏 **100 Claim Blocks**

This helps keep claims organized across the world.

---

## Creating a Claim

Use the Golden Shovel provided by GriefPrevention.

Simply:

1. Right-click one corner.
2. Right-click the opposite corner.

Your land is now protected.


---

## Managing Claims

The easiest way to manage your claims is:

```text
/claims
```

This opens a simple menu where you can:

- View Claims
- Teleport
- Resize Claims
- Manage Trust
- View Information


---

## Buying More Claim Blocks

Need more space?

Purchase additional Claim Blocks using:

```text
/buyclaim
```

Price:

💰 **$300 per Claim Block**

---

## Trusting Friends

You can allow other players to build inside your claim.

Use:

```text
/trust <player>
```

You remain the owner and can remove access at any time.

---

!!! tip

    Claim your base as soon as possible to avoid losing your progress.
