# Custom Features

ArteryX adds a collection of quality-of-life features that improve survival without changing the vanilla experience.

---

## 🌳 Fast Leaf Decay

Leaves decay much faster after breaking a tree, making wood farming quicker.

---

## 🏗️ Concrete Cauldron

Right-click **Concrete Powder** on a **Water Cauldron** to instantly create Concrete.

---

## 🌱 Mud Cauldron

Right-click **Dirt** on a Water Cauldron to convert it into Mud.

---

## 🟧 Copper Cauldrons

Special cauldrons make working with Copper easier.

- 🟢 Slime Cauldron → Ages Copper
- 🍯 Honey Cauldron → Waxes Copper

---

## 🐉 Dragon Rewards

Every Ender Dragon has a **25% chance** to drop an Elytra.

---

## 🖼 Better Paintings

Scroll through painting variants before placing them.

---

## 🖌 Invisible Item Frames

Use a **Brush** on an Item Frame to toggle its visibility.

---

## 🤫 Silent Mobs

Rename any mob:

```
Silence Me
```

or

```
silence_me
```

to permanently silence it.

---

## 🧪 XP Bottling

Store your experience using Glass Bottles.

- Stores 10 Levels
- Returns 7 Levels when broken
- Sneak to bottle multiple at once

---

## 🪚 Wooden Stonecutter

The Stonecutter also works as a Sawmill, allowing more efficient wooden recipes.

---

## 📖 Auto Unlock Recipes

All crafting recipes are unlocked automatically when you join.

---

## 🐺 Transfer Pets

Safely transfer ownership of tamed pets to another player.

---

## 🐣 Keep Baby Animals Small

Name a baby mob:

```
Keep Small
```

Rename it:

```
Grow
```

to let it become an adult.

---

## 🧱 Mini Blocks

Wandering Traders sell decorative Mini Blocks for builders.

---

## 💛 Vanilla, But Better

Every feature is designed to save time, reduce repetitive tasks, and improve survival while keeping the gameplay feeling close to vanilla Minecraft.
