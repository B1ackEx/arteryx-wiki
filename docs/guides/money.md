# Making Money

Whether you're just starting or building a massive empire, there are plenty of ways to earn money on ArteryX.

---

## 🌾 Farming

Build crop farms and sell your harvest using:

- `/shop`
- Sell Wands

The larger your farm, the more money you'll earn.

---

## ⛏️ Mining

Explore caves and gather valuable ores.

Sell your resources at:

```text
/shop
```

or keep them for crafting and progression.

---

## 🐄 Mob Farms

Mob farms are one of the best long-term money makers.

Upgrade your farm with:

- **Forge Spawners**
- **Flux Hoppers**
- **Sell Wands**

These three features work together to create a nearly automatic income.

---

## ⚡ Flux Hoppers

Flux Hoppers automatically collect nearby drops and move them into linked containers.

Perfect for:

- Mob Farms
- Crop Farms
- Automatic Collection

Less manual work means more profit.

---

## 🪄 Sell Wands

Sell Wands instantly sell everything inside a chest.

Simply:

1. Hold a Sell Wand.
2. Right-click a container.
3. Everything is sold instantly.

No moving items.
No opening the shop.
Just instant money.

---

## 🏪 Player Shops

Think your items are worth more than `/shop`?

Create your own player shop and sell directly to other players.

Popular resources often sell for much higher prices.

---

## ⚖️ Auction House

Sell rare or valuable items using:

```text
/ah
```

The Auction House lets players bid or purchase items from one another.

Perfect for:

- Rare Gear
- Enchanted Items
- Resources
- Collectibles

---

## 📈 Shop Prices

The Server Shop is **not completely static**.

Some item prices may be:

- 📈 Buffed
- 📉 Nerfed

over time to help balance the economy and encourage players to explore different ways of making money.

---

## 💡 Best Money Setup

For the highest income, combine:

- Forge Spawners
- Flux Hoppers
- Sell Wands

This creates a fast, efficient, and mostly automatic farming system.

---

!!! tip

    Don't rely on just one income source. Farming, mining, player shops, and automatic farms together are the fastest way to become wealthy on ArteryX.
