# Server Rules

Play fair, respect others, and help keep ArteryX enjoyable for everyone.

!!! warning "Important"

    Joining ArteryX means you agree to follow **all** of the rules below. Staff decisions may be made on situations not explicitly listed if required to protect the server.

---

# General Rules

### Respect Everyone

- Treat all players and staff with respect.
- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or personal attacks are not allowed.
- Do not impersonate staff or other players.

---

### Fair Gameplay

- Do not exploit bugs or glitches.
- Report exploits instead of abusing them.
- Modified clients that provide an unfair advantage are prohibited.

Examples include:

- X-Ray
- Kill Aura
- Reach
- Auto Crystal
- Fly Hacks
- Speed Hacks
- Freecam
- Auto Mine
- Inventory Tweaks that automate gameplay

---

### Griefing & Theft

- Respect claims and player builds.
- Do not intentionally destroy community projects.
- Scamming players is not allowed.

---

### Alternate Accounts

- Using alternate accounts to gain unfair advantages is prohibited.
- Staff may limit or remove alternate accounts when necessary.

---

# Chat Rules

### Keep Chat Friendly

- No excessive swearing.
- No hate speech or discrimination.
- No harassment or bullying.
- No inappropriate or NSFW content.
- No political or religious arguments.

---

### Spam

Do not:

- Spam messages.
- Flood chat.
- Abuse CAPS.
- Repeatedly advertise the same message.

---

### Advertising

Advertising is prohibited, including:

- Other Minecraft servers
- Discord servers
- Websites
- Social media intended to recruit players elsewhere

---

# Discord Rules

All Minecraft rules also apply inside the Discord server.

Additionally:

- No ping spam.
- No inappropriate usernames or profile pictures.
- Do not abuse tickets.
- Keep discussions in the correct channels.
- Follow staff instructions.

---

# Economy Rules

The economy is meant to stay fair.

The following are prohibited:

- Duplication glitches.
- Exploiting economy bugs.
- Chargeback fraud.
- Abuse of AFK or automation systems.

---

# PvP Rules

PvP is allowed.

However:

- Combat logging is prohibited.
- Exploiting PvP bugs is prohibited.
- Intentionally crashing players or the server is prohibited.

---

# Staff Rules

Staff decisions are final.

If you believe a punishment was incorrect:

- Stay respectful.
- Create a Discord ticket.
- Do not argue publicly.

---

# Punishment Guidelines

| Offense | Typical Punishment |
|:---------|:-------------------|
| Chat Spam | Warning → Mute |
| Excessive Swearing | Warning → Mute |
| Toxic Behavior | Mute / Temporary Ban |
| Advertising | Temporary Ban |
| X-Ray / Hacks | Permanent Ban |
| Duplication Exploits | Permanent Ban |
| Ban Evasion | Permanent Ban |
| Chargebacks | Permanent Ban |
| Staff Impersonation | Temporary Ban |
| Inappropriate Builds | Removal + Punishment |
| Exploit Abuse | Temporary or Permanent Ban |

!!! note

    Punishments may be increased depending on severity or repeated offenses.
