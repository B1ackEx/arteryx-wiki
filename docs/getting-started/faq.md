# Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about ArteryX.

---

## Can I trade paid ranks for in-game money?

**Yes.**

Players may trade paid ranks with other players using **in-game money**, as long as:

- Both players agree to the trade.
- The price is agreed upon by both players.
- No real-money payment is involved.

!!! note

    ArteryX staff are not responsible for player-to-player trades. Trade carefully.

---

## How do I earn money?

There are many ways to earn money on ArteryX:

- OneBlock progression
- Farming
- Mining
- Mob farming
- Selling items
- Player Shops
- Auction House
- Voting
- Events
- Custom server features

---

## How does OneBlock work?

Every player gets their own **OneBlock island**.

Break your OneBlock to progress through different phases and unlock new resources, blocks, and content.

Use:

```
/ob

to open the main OneBlock menu.

Useful commands include:

/ob upgrade
/ob phases
/ob settings
/ob warp
/ob level
```

---

## Can I upgrade my island?

**Yes.**

Use:

```text
/ob upgrade
```

to upgrade your island size.

Your island starts at:

**50 × 50**

and can eventually be upgraded up to:

**250 × 250**

---

## Can I change my OneBlock phase?

**Yes.**

Use:

```text
/ob phases
```

You can switch between phases that you have already unlocked.

---

## Are there Nether and End islands?

**Yes.**

ArteryX includes **Nether and End OneBlock islands**.

Both use the same island size system as your main island and are generated as **void-style islands**.

---

## Can I change my island settings?

Yes.

Use:

```text
/ob settings
```

to view and configure the available island settings.

---

## Can I teleport to my island?

Yes.

Use:

```text
/ob warp
```

to access your available island warps.

---

## Can I check my island level?

Yes.

Use:

```text
/ob level
```

to view your island level and progression.

---

## Is Keep Inventory enabled?

**No.**

Keep Inventory is **disabled** on ArteryX.

Death can cause you to lose the items you were carrying, so keep valuable items stored safely.

---

## Can I PvP?

**Yes.**

PvP is allowed where enabled.

Keep Inventory is disabled, so PvP deaths can result in item loss.

---

## Is teleport killing allowed?

**Yes.**

Teleport killing is allowed on ArteryX.

If you accept a teleport request or teleport somewhere dangerous, you are responsible for the outcome.

---

## Is trapping allowed?

**Yes.**

Player trapping is allowed using normal gameplay mechanics.

However, exploits, cheats, or server-crashing methods are not allowed.

---

## Can I AFK?

**Yes.**

AFKing is allowed.

However, using **auto-clickers, bots, macros, or other automation** to perform gameplay actions is not allowed.

---

## Can I use mods?

**Yes.**

Client-side mods are allowed as long as they do not provide an unfair gameplay advantage or automate gameplay.

Generally allowed:

* Sodium
* Iris
* OptiFine
* Shaders
* Replay Mod
* Performance mods
* Cosmetic mods
* Client-side UI mods
* Minimap mods without unfair features

Not allowed:

* X-Ray
* Kill Aura
* Reach
* Fly Hacks
* Speed Hacks
* Auto Mine
* Auto Clickers
* Combat automation
* Gameplay automation
* Any modification that provides an unfair advantage

If you are unsure about a mod, ask staff before using it.

---

## How do I get Crate Keys?

Crate Keys can be obtained through:

* Voting
* VoteParty

* Special rewards

## What happens if I get banned?

If you believe your punishment was incorrect, contact staff through Discord and open a support ticket.

Do **not** attempt to evade your punishment using another account.

---

## I found a bug. What should I do?

Report it privately to staff through Discord.

Do not abuse or share serious exploits.
