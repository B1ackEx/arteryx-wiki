# Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about ArteryX.

---

## Can I trade paid ranks for in-game money?

**Yes.**

Players are free to trade paid ranks with other players **using in-game money**, as long as:

- The owner of the rank agrees to the trade.
- The price is decided by the seller.
- No real money is involved.

!!! note

    ArteryX staff are **not responsible** for player-to-player trades. Always trade carefully.


---

## How do I earn money?

There are many ways to earn money, including:

- Farming
- Mining
- Selling items
- Player Shops
- Vote Rewards
- Auction House
- Custom Features

See the **Making Money** guide for more information.

---

## How do I protect my base?

Use **Claim Blocks** to create protected land.

Visit the **Claim Blocks Guide** to learn how to claim and expand your territory.

---

## How do I get more Claim Blocks?

You can earn Claim Blocks by:

- /buyclaim

---

## How do I get Crate Keys?

Crate Keys can be obtained through:

- VoteParty
- Events
- Giveaways
- Special Rewards

---

## Can players steal from my claim?

No.

Players cannot break or place blocks inside your claim unless you give them permission.

---

## What happens if I get banned?

If you believe your punishment was incorrect, create a support ticket in our Discord.

Do **not** evade your ban or use alternate accounts.

---

## I found a bug. What should I do?

Please report it privately to the staff through Discord.

Players who abuse exploits may receive severe punishments.

---

## Can I use mods?

Allowed:

- OptiFine
- Sodium
- Iris
- Replay Mod
- Shaders
- Minimap (without cave mapping)
- Client-Side Mods

Not Allowed:

- X-Ray
- Kill Aura
- Fly Hacks
- Reach
- Auto Mine
- Any unfair gameplay modifications

---

## Can I AFK?

Yes.

AFKing is allowed as long as you are **not abusing** bugs, exploits, or unauthorized automation.

---
